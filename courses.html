Courses that teach the skills the job postings ask for, grouped by track. Start with a free option, then go deeper.

Checked on each provider's own page on Oct 27, 2026. Prices and formats change, so confirm before you enroll. No affiliate links.

AI drug discovery

TeachOpenCADD

Volkamer Lab

For anyone who wants to learn computer-aided drug design by running real code, step by step.

FreeAimed at newcomers with a biology/chemistry or computational backgroundInteractive Jupyter notebooks (talktorials) using open-source Python packages such as RDKit

DeepChem tutorials

DeepChem (open-source project)

For Python users who want hands-on deep learning for chemistry, drug discovery and biology.

FreeStarts beginner-friendly (assumes you are new to ML and life sciences), then moves to advanced topicsNotebooks designed for Google Colab (can also run locally)

Drug Discovery

University of California San Diego (Skaggs School of Pharmacy) on CourseraAbout 9 hours

For people new to pharma who want a plain overview of how drugs are discovered.

Free to start; certificate costs extraBeginnerSelf-paced online course, 6 modules

Artificial Intelligence in Pharma and Biotech

MIT Sloan School of Management, delivered by GetSmarter (2U)6 weeks, 6-8 hours per week

For pharma and biotech professionals and leaders who want a business-level view of AI, not coding.

$3,250Online, cohort-based; videos, assignments, quizzes, forums

Computational biology and genomics

Rosalind

UC San Diego and Saint Petersburg Academic University (with the Russian Academy of Sciences)

For biologists learning to code and programmers learning biology, one solved problem at a time.

FreeProblems range from easy to hardOnline problem-solving platform with automatic grading

Galaxy Training Network

Galaxy community

For scientists who want to analyze sequencing and other data without writing code.

FreeAll levels, from beginners to experienced researchersHands-on online tutorials and slides for the Galaxy platform

6.047 Computational Biology (Fall 2015)

MIT OpenCourseWare (Prof. Manolis Kellis)

For learners with some algorithms and probability background who want a rigorous university course in genomics algorithms.

FreeUndergraduate; prerequisites are intro algorithms, probability and intro biologyOpen course materials: syllabus, lecture slides, open textbook, problem sets, projects

Bioinformatics Specialization

University of California San Diego on CourseraListed as 3 months at 10 hours per week; FAQ says most learners finish in 4-6 months

For beginners who want to learn the core algorithms of bioinformatics from the ground up.

Coursera subscriptionBeginnerSelf-paced online, 7 courses, optional Python coding Honors Track

For anyone analyzing single-cell data who wants current, benchmarked best practices.

FreeNewcomers through experienced researchersFree online book with code examples, updated as tools change
