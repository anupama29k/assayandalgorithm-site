For people who want to build a bio×AI company, or join one early: accelerators, founder fellowships, non-dilutive funding and lab space, plus who is hiring.

Terms and deadlines are quoted from each program's official page, checked Oct 27, 2026.

Accelerators

Y Combinator

Winter 2027: Nov 2, 2026, 8 pm PT · Open; late applications considered

What you get: $500,000 standard deal: $125,000 on a post-money SAFE for 7%, plus $375,000 on an uncapped MFN SAFE; no fees

Berkeley SkyDeck

Batch 24 applications open in January · Opens January

What you get: $210,000 investment from the Berkeley SkyDeck Fund for cohort teams; about 20 startups selected every six months.

Who can apply: Startups from anywhere, in any industry; international and UC-affiliated startups get priority

SOSV (IndieBio)

Apply online · Applications open

What you get: IndieBio, SOSV's biotech accelerator, with programs in New York and San Francisco. See SOSV for investment terms.

Who can apply: Pre-seed deep tech (including life sciences) with 2+ founders who can join an in-person U.S. program in New York or San Francisco

LabCentral AI BioHub Accelerator

Next cohort not yet announced · Between cohorts

What you get: A 10–12 week program for AI-first biotech founders, co-led with C10 Labs: wet lab space, AI research tools, mentors and investors.

Who can apply: Pre-seed to seed AI + life science founders focused on human health, who need wet lab access soon and can be on site in Kendall Square at least one day a week

Founder fellowships

Activate Fellowship (U.S.)

Oct 30, 12 pm PT (U.S. Cohort 2027) · Open

What you get: $100K a year living stipend, $100K in R&D support, $12K a year for travel and development, plus health insurance and relocation stipends.

Who can apply: First-time hardware founders with a bachelor's degree and 4+ years of research experience, working full time, able to work in the U.S.; projects that only serve healthcare or need FDA approval are not supported

Entrepreneur First (U.S.)

Rolling · Open

What you get: Up to $250K ($125K for 8% on a post-money SAFE, plus an optional $125K uncapped MFN SAFE), or a $10K equity-free residency for founders still exploring.

Who can apply: Individuals, with or without a cofounder or idea; any sector; based in San Francisco

Grants and non-dilutive funding

NSF SBIR/STTR (America's Seed Fund)

Project Pitch accepted anytime · Open

What you get: Non-dilutive federal R&D funding for startups (Phase I, then Phase II). Submit a Project Pitch first; NSF replies in about 1–2 months and invites full proposals.

Who can apply: U.S. small businesses with fewer than 500 employees; PI at least 51% employed by the company; at least 50% owned by U.S. citizens or permanent residents

NIH SBIR/STTR

Next standard due date: Jan 5, 2027 (then Apr 5 and Sep 5) · Open

What you get: Non-dilutive NIH funding for small businesses in phases. See NIH for current award sizes.

Who can apply: U.S. small businesses; for SBIR the PI's main employment (more than 50%) must be with the company

ARPA-H

Rolling (Mission Office ISOs) · Open

What you get: Funding for health research and development. Send a short Solution Summary first and wait for written feedback before a full proposal.

Who can apply: Individuals, small businesses and other for-profit groups can apply (some exclusions)

Convergent Research (Focused Research Organizations)

Ideas accepted any time · Open

What you get: Support to launch a Focused Research Organization: a new nonprofit built around one roughly five-year project.

Who can apply: Scientists and technical teams with a big, time-bound project that does not fit an academic lab or a startup

Lab space

BioLabs Golden Tickets

Varies by sponsor · No open competitions right now

What you get: One year of BioLabs membership and a dedicated lab bench (some sponsors add a desk and mentoring)

Who can apply: Early-stage life science startups; each sponsor sets its own focus areas and terms

LabCentral

Online application · No deadlines listed

What you get: Shared lab space (243,000 sq ft listed), 5,000+ pieces of shared equipment, and operations support. Golden Tickets: a $50,000 credit at 700 Main Street.

Who can apply: Early- to mid-stage human health companies; for 700 Main Street, no more than $10M raised or $3M revenue in the past 12 months

Where to search for startup roles

  • YC Work at a Startup: Jobs at Y Combinator-backed startups

  • Wellfound: Startup jobs across roles, often with salary and equity shown

  • BioSpace: Biotech, pharma and clinical research jobs

  • ISCB Career Center: Computational biology and bioinformatics jobs; you can also post a CV

  • CompBioJobs: Industry jobs in computational biology, bioinformatics and biostatistics, refreshed daily

  • jobRxiv: International science jobs, free for candidates

  • Nature Careers: Academic and industry science jobs, including postdocs and faculty posts
