Fellowships for academics

Fellowships and grants for PhD students, postdocs and early faculty working in computational or quantitative biology.

Support and deadlines are quoted from each program's official page, checked Oct 27, 2026. Programs change their rules each cycle, so read the official page before applying.

Open now or coming up

Paul & Daisy Soros Fellowships for New Americans

PhD applicant / early graduate studentDeadline: Oct 29, 2026, 2:00 pm ETupcoming (deadline in 2 days; the page lists the deadline but does not explicitly say 'open')

Immigrants or children of immigrants (naturalized citizens, green card holders, asylees/refugees, or U.S. high school and college graduates including DACA), age 30 or younger at the deadline. Must have a bachelor's by fall 2027 and enroll full-time in an eligible U.S. graduate program in 2027-28. Current grad students must not have started their third year.

Support: Up to $25,000 per year stipend plus 50% of tuition and fees (up to $10,000 per semester / $6,667 per quarter). Total stipend from all sources is capped at $50,000 per year.

Field: any graduate or professional discipline, including STEM and computational biology. The number of years of support was not stated on the page fetched.

Hertz Foundation Fellowship

PhD applicant / first-year PhD studentDeadline: Oct 30, 2026, 11:59 pm Pacific (recommender forms due Nov 2, 2026, 6:00 pm Pacific)open now (application opened Aug 31, 2026)

U.S. citizens or permanent residents who are college seniors, first-year graduate students, or in a gap year, intending to pursue a PhD in applied physical and biological sciences, mathematics, or engineering. Recipients must be full-time at a participating school.

Support: Up to five years of support, including a stipend and full tuition equivalent.

Field: applied physical and biological sciences, math, engineering. The opening date and the up-to-five-years term are also on https://www.hertzfoundation.org/news/2027-hertz-fellowship-application-now-open/. No stipend dollar figure is stated on either page.

SLAS Graduate Education Fellowship

PhD studentDeadline: Nov 9, 2026open now

One graduate student enrolled in a program related to high-throughput screening, quantitative biosciences or drug discovery, nominated through their institution.

Support: Up to $100,000 total, paid as up to $50,000 a year for two years.

Damon Runyon Quantitative Biology Fellowship

postdocDeadline: Dec 1, 2026, 4:00 pm ET (via Altum ProposalCentral)upcoming (the page does not say whether submission is open yet)

Computational scientists (PhD, MD, MD/PhD, DDS, DVM, DO) whose degree was conferred no more than 5 years before the deadline. Requires two committed mentors (one dry-lab, one wet-lab cancer biology). Applicants must not have been in their mentors' labs for more than 24 months. Non-U.S. citizens must train at a U.S. institution.

Support: Three-year award: $76,000 (Year 1), $78,000 (Year 2), $80,000 (Year 3) stipend, plus $2,000 per year expense allowance.

Field: quantitative biology and data science applied to cancer research. Aimed at people trained in math, CS, physics, or engineering. Current or awarded NIH K-award holders are ineligible.

NIH Ruth L. Kirschstein NRSA Individual Fellowships (F31 predoctoral / F32 postdoctoral)

PhD student (F31) / postdoc (F32)Deadline: Dec 8, 2026, 5:00 pm local time (standard F-series cycle III; later dates Apr 8, 2027 and Aug 8, 2027)upcoming (recurring three times a year: April 8, August 8, December 8)

U.S. citizen, non-citizen national, or permanent resident by time of award. F31: predoctoral students doing dissertation research with a sponsor. F32: doctoral degree required before activation, full-time training, sponsor identified before applying.

Support: F32: up to three years of postdoctoral support (stipend, tuition and fees, institutional allowance); total NRSA postdoc support is capped at 3 years. F31: null (no amount or duration confirmed on the official pages fetched).

Field: any NIH-mission biomedical research, including computational biology. F32 is Parent NOFO PA-25-423 (expires 2028-05-08). F31 is Parent NOFO PA-25-422; its closing date of 2028-05-07 comes from https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/358848, and the F-series standard dates come from https://grants.nih.gov/node/1091. The full PA-25-422 text could not be fetched, so F31 support terms are null. Individual NIH institutes set their own rules.

HHMI Hanna H. Gray Fellows Program (2028 competition, combined with Freeman Hrabowski Scholars)

postdoc (postdoc-to-faculty transition)Deadline: Dec 15, 2026, 11:59 pm ET (applications open Nov 3, 2026, 9:00 am ET)upcoming (opens Nov 3, 2026; the competition runs every other year)

PhD and/or MD with no more than 7 years of postdoctoral experience as of 2026-12-15, doing basic biological or biomedical research in a mentored postdoc at an HHMI-eligible institution. Must plan to start a tenure-track position at an eligible institution by 2030-05-01 and be authorized (or able to obtain authorization) to work in the U.S.

Support: Salary or stipend commensurate with postdoctoral experience plus expense support, for up to two years (one-year term, renewable once). No dollar amount is stated on the page.

Field: basic biological and biomedical research. Appointments begin 2028-05-01 to 2028-08-31.

DOE Computational Science Graduate Fellowship (DOE CSGF)

PhD applicant / early PhD studentDeadline: Jan 7, 2027 (37th cohort, 2027-28 incoming class)upcoming (the page does not explicitly say the application is open yet)

Undergraduate seniors, master's students and first-year PhD students in computational science; not second-year or later graduate students.

Support: Four years of support, renewed annually. See the program site for stipend and tuition details.

Field: high-performance computing applied to science and engineering, including computational biology. Benefits are from https://krellinst.org/csgf/about-doe-csgf/benefits-opportunities, eligibility from https://krellinst.org/csgf/about-doe-csgf/news-events/apply-now. The exact career-stage limits (for example, first-year grad students) were not confirmed.

Jane Coffin Childs Memorial Fund Postdoctoral Fellowship

postdoc (or final-year PhD candidate)Deadline: Feb 1, 2027open now

2027 round: no more than 12 months of postdoc at the deadline (first postdoc started on or after 2026-02-01), PhD conferred on or after 2025-02-01, or PhD candidates who will finish before the fellowship starts. A sponsoring lab is required. Any nationality, but non-U.S. citizens must use U.S. host institutions.

Field: medical and biomedical research (the field is not specified further on the page). Support terms are from https://www.jccfund.org/?p=301 (Terms of the Award).

EMBL EIPOD-LinC (Exploring Life in Context) Postdoctoral Fellowship

postdocDeadline: Feb 1, 2027, 23:59 CET (application opens Nov 25, 2026; info webinar Nov 19, 2026)upcoming (opens Nov 25, 2026)

PhD by 2027-02-01 (defended but not yet awarded is OK), up to 8 years of research experience since the PhD, any nationality. No more than 12 months of prior EMBL/Member State association in the past 3 years. Requires an EMBL host plus an external partner lab in an EMBL Member State (3-month to 1-year secondment).

Support: Three-year, fully funded contract at the host EMBL site (salary amount not stated). Co-funded by Horizon Europe MSCA.

Field: interdisciplinary 'Molecules to Ecosystems' projects. Theme 4 'AI and Theory' covers mathematical models, physics, and AI for structural biology, omics, and imaging. Eligible backgrounds include math, physics, and engineering. Interviews are 2027-04-08 to 2027-04-09; latest start is 2027-09-01.

NIH Pathway to Independence Award (K99/R00)

postdoc (postdoc-to-faculty transition)Deadline: Feb 12, 2027 (next new-application date; resubmissions Nov 12, 2026), 5:00 pm local timeupcoming (standard K-series dates: Feb 12, Jun 12, Oct 12 for new applications)

Postdoctoral researchers with no more than 4 years of postdoctoral research experience as of the relevant application due date.

Support: K99: up to 2 years of mentored support (salary and research costs at the awarding Institute's levels). R00: up to 3 years, total cost not to exceed $249,000 per year. Total project period of up to 5 years.

Field: any NIH-mission research. This is Parent NOFO PA-24-194 (independent clinical trial not allowed), which expires 2027-05-08. Check for a reissued NOFO after that date. Standard dates are confirmed on https://grants.nih.gov/node/1091.

Arc Institute Science Fellows

early independent researcher (straight from PhD/MD)Deadline: rolling (no deadline listed; reviewed until filled)open now (Arc plans to hire 1-2 Science Fellows in 2026)

Completed or expected to complete a PhD, MD, or MD/PhD between spring 2024 and summer 2026. U.S.-authorized candidates and those needing visa sponsorship may both apply.

Support: Annual direct-cost budget supporting a team of 2-4 people (separate from salary and benefits; no dollar amount stated). Five-year term with a formal check-in after year three.

Field: computational science/ML, chemical biology, human genetics, immunology, neurobiology, technology development. In vivo immunology programs are especially encouraged. This is an independent PI-style position, not a traditional postdoc.

Closed this cycle: watch for the next call

NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program (GRFP)

PhD applicant / early PhD studentDeadline: Oct 19, 2026 (Life Sciences); Oct 20, 2026 (Computer & Information Sciences & Engineering); Oct 22, 2026 (Engineering); Oct 23, 2026 (Mathematical Sciences, Chemistry, Physics, Geosciences). All 8:00 pm ET. Reference letters were due Oct 16, 2026.closed until next cycle (all 2026 field deadlines have passed; the next window is not stated on the solicitation)

U.S. citizens, nationals or permanent residents who are undergraduate seniors, bachelor's holders with no graduate enrollment, or first-year graduate students (less than one academic year completed) in a research-based master's or PhD program.

Support: $37,000 stipend for a 12-month tenure plus $16,000 cost-of-education allowance per tenure year (up to $53,000 per Fellow per year); three years of support usable within a five-year fellowship period.

Field: all NSF-supported STEM, including Life Sciences, Bioinformatics (under CISE), and Mathematical Biology/Biostatistics (under Mathematical Sciences). Research directly tied to human disease, diagnosis, treatment, drug development, or animal models for testing treatments is ineligible. Solicitation NSF 26-526.

Google PhD Fellowship

PhD studentDeadline: Apr 30, 2026 (2026 cycle; opened Mar 5, 2026)closed until next cycle (the 2026 cycle is closed; the next window is not stated)

Full-time PhD students in computer science or an adjacent field, nominated by their university (no direct applications). U.S./Canada: must have completed graduate coursework by the award year, with up to 4 nominees per university. Past awardees, and students with comparable industry awards, are ineligible.

Support: U.S./Canada: up to 2 years, US $85K per year (effective 2025 for new recipients). Other regions vary (e.g., India early-stage up to 4 years at US $50K).

Field: computing research areas. The 'Health Research' area covers complex medical data, including genomics. There is no dedicated computational biology area. Internal university nomination deadlines come earlier than Google's.

Burroughs Wellcome Fund Career Awards at the Scientific Interface (CASI)

postdoc (postdoc-to-faculty transition)Deadline: LOI Aug 14, 2026, 3 pm EDT (passed); full application (by invitation, invites Nov 13, 2026) Jan 11, 2027, 3 pm ESTclosed until next cycle (LOI deadline passed; the page reads 'Not Open to Applications'; the next LOI window is not stated)

U.S./Canadian citizens, permanent residents, or temporary residents with qualifying visas. PhD in math, physics, chemistry, CS, statistics, or engineering (biology PhDs only with prior Program Officer approval). Generally 12 to 60 months of postdoc, at least one first-author paper, at a U.S./Canadian non-profit. Current or accepted K99/R00 holders and tenure-track faculty are ineligible.

Support: $560,000 over five years (bridging advanced postdoc and the first three years of faculty service).

Field: physical, mathematical, and computational scientists or engineers moving into biology, with non-biological approaches. Awards start 2027-07-01.

Schmidt Science Fellows

PhD student (final year) / new postdocDeadline: Jul 13, 2026 (2027-cohort full application; institutional nominations were due May 15, 2026)closed until next cycle (2027-cohort deadlines have passed; the next cycle is not stated)

Must be nominated by a nominating-partner PhD institution. For the 2027 cohort, PhD requirements (including the defense) must be completed between 2026-05-01 and 2027-06-30. PhD must be in natural sciences, engineering, mathematics, or computing.

Field: interdisciplinary 'pivot' postdoc across natural sciences, engineering, math, and computing. Eligibility is from https://schmidtsciencefellows.org/selection/who-can-apply/. The support amount is not stated on the pages fetched. The 2027 cohort will be announced in March 2027.

Life Sciences Research Foundation (LSRF) Postdoctoral Fellowship

postdocDeadline: Oct 1, 2026, 8:00 pm ET (reference letters due Nov 1, 2026)closed until next cycle (the 2026-27 deadline has passed; the next cycle date is not stated)

Within 5 years of PhD/MD/DVM as of 2026-10-01. Must begin the proposed postdoc lab between 2025-08-01 and 2027-07-31, in a lab different from the thesis lab. Any nationality (non-U.S. citizens must work in a U.S. lab). Postdocs in HHMI Investigator labs are ineligible this cycle.

Support: Three years: $79,000 per year salary ($237,000 total) plus $6,000 per year research allowance ($18,000 total), for fellowships beginning on or after 2026-08-01.

Field: basic life sciences (no discipline restriction stated). Award terms are from https://lsrf.org/apply/award-details/.

Helen Hay Whitney Foundation Postdoctoral Research Fellowship

postdoc (or final-year PhD candidate)Deadline: Jun 15, 2026, 5:00 pm EST (for activation Apr 1, 2027 to Sep 1, 2027)closed until next cycle (the 2026 deadline has passed; the next deadline is not stated)

PhD defended no more than 2 years before the deadline (MD no more than 4 years), no more than 1 year in a postdoctoral lab at the deadline. No citizenship requirement (non-U.S. applicants must obtain a visa). Training must be in an academic or non-profit setting.

Support: Three years: $73,000 (Year 1), $75,000 (Year 2), $77,000 (Year 3) stipend, plus $1,500 per year research allowance.

Field: basic biomedical sciences (examples given: neuroscience, structural biology, developmental biology). Eligibility details are from the 2027 Application FAQ: https://hhwf.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/ApplicationFAQ2027.V.1.pdf.
